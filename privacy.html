Privacy Policy

WiFi Odds for Flights browser extension and the wifiodds.com website

Effective July 24, 2026

The short version: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no cookies, and no server of our own. Nothing about you is collected, stored, sold, or shared — by the extension or by this site. The only network requests the extension makes on your behalf are anonymous route or flight-number lookups to public community trackers.

What the extension does

It shows the historical probability that a given flight is operated by an aircraft with modern high-speed satellite WiFi (Starlink, Amazon Leo) rather than legacy service — as colour-coded badges on airline search results, and as a ConnectScore in its popup.

Data collection: none

The extension and this website do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no analytics, no tracking pixels, no accounts, no sign-in, and no server we operate that receives your data.

What actually happens

  • Reading pages, locally. When you view a flight search on united.com, alaskaair.com, or Navan, the extension reads the route and flight numbers already displayed on the page you are on. That parsing happens entirely in your browser; nothing about the page is uploaded anywhere.
  • Odds lookups. To attach odds to a flight, the extension requests statistics from the public APIs of the independent community trackers unitedstarlinktracker.com and alaskastarlinktracker.com. Those requests contain only a route (for example "DEN to SFO"), or a flight number and a date. They contain no personal information, no booking details, no passenger names, no fare or payment data, and nothing that identifies you or your account.
  • Caching, locally. Responses are cached for about six hours in your browser's local extension storage, alongside a small number of UI preferences (panel collapsed, keep-sorted, and whether you have enabled Alaska support). This data never leaves your machine, and clearing the extension's storage or uninstalling it removes it.
  • Selector updates. Roughly once a day the extension fetches a small configuration file from wifiodds.com that describes where the relevant elements sit on the airline pages, so a site redesign does not break it. That request carries no data about you beyond what any web request necessarily reveals (your IP address, to the hosting provider), and it is not logged or joined to anything.
  • This website. wifiodds.com is a static site served by Cloudflare Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and embeds no third-party scripts or trackers. Cloudflare processes standard, short-lived request logs as our hosting provider; we neither read nor retain them.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • united.com content script — to display odds badges on United search results.
  • alaskaair.com (optional) — off by default. Grant it only if you want Alaska badges; the extension registers that content script at the moment you enable it, and stops when you revoke it.
  • Navan content script — to display badges on Navan corporate-travel search results.
  • unitedstarlinktracker.com, alaskastarlinktracker.com — to fetch route and flight statistics.
  • wifiodds.com — the daily selector-configuration file described above.
  • storage — the local cache and preferences described above.
  • activeTab — so the popup can read the route of the tab you invoke it on.
  • scripting — to register the optional Alaska content script when, and only when, you grant that permission.

Children

The extension and the site are general-audience tools that collect no data at all, and therefore collect no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the effective date above changes with it and the previous version stays in the public git history of the repository below.
