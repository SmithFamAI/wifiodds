The playbook

What to do about it

Four things, in the order they become useful. Every one of them is available at tier D, which is the tier this page was derived at. Anything this fleet cannot support has been cut from the list.

  • T-48hRe-check about two days out

    Aircraft assignments firm up roughly 48 hours before departure and they can still move up to pushback. Whatever this page says today, the answer for your flight is more settled then.

  • AmenityRead the airline’s own WiFi flag at T-48h

    American publishes an amenity marker against each flight in its own booking flow, and that marker is about the aircraft now assigned to it. It is the closest thing to a per-flight answer this fleet has. The airline is asserting it; nobody has verified the install.

  • FallbackHave the offline version of the work

    Nobody has load-tested a full cabin on any of these systems, so a good score is not a promise about the hour you fly. Downloading the deck is the move that makes the odds stop mattering.

American WiFi questions

Does American have Viasat WiFi?

American has Viasat on 890 of its 989 aircraft (90%) as of 2026-07, so it is close to a sure thing. Its ConnectScore is 51 out of 100, in the mixed band.

Is American’s WiFi free?

On American it is free onboard. ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor, so a paid or unconfirmed free claim scores lower than the same fleet given away free.
