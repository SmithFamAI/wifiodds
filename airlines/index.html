Airline WiFi questions

methodology →

Which airline has the best inflight WiFi right now?

JSX (ConnectScore 100/100, good), then ZIPAIR (ConnectScore 100/100, good) then airBaltic (ConnectScore 100/100, good), as of 2026-07-27. ConnectScore is the chance of getting the good, modern system on a random flight, multiplied by whether it is free once you are onboard, so a small all-Starlink fleet can outrank a giant airline that is only part way through its rollout.

Which airlines have Starlink WiFi?

15 of the 18 airlines here fly Starlink on at least part of the fleet: JSX (56 of 56), ZIPAIR (8 of 8), airBaltic (28 of 55), WestJet (100 of 193), Hawaiian (42 of 66), Qatar Airways (120 of 241), Alaska (99 of 350), United (483 of 1,807), Virgin Atlantic (12 of 43), Emirates (36 of 232), Air France (229 aircraft, count unpublished), Air Canada (12 of 216), Southwest (1 of 803), British Airways (5 of 261), SAS (123 aircraft, count unpublished). Fleet share is what matters. Being on the list is not the same as being likely on your flight.

Which airlines give inflight WiFi away free?

Free for everyone onboard, no loyalty program and no purchase: JSX, ZIPAIR, airBaltic, WestJet, Hawaiian, Qatar Airways, jetBlue, Alaska, American, Delta, Emirates, Air France, British Airways. Others are free only for members or only on some cabins, and that scores lower: ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor instead of treating a paywalled system as the same product.

How is ConnectScore calculated?

ConnectScore = the sum, over every segment of the fleet, of fleet share × system quality × free-for-you. The published score is the floor. Data: unitedstarlinktracker.com · alaskastarlinktracker.com · airline announcements (Jul 2026). Worked example. United flies five systems, so it gets five rows: Starlink 483 aircraft at 30.6 points, Viasat 525 aircraft at 12.8 points, Panasonic 407 aircraft at 4.0 points, Thales FlytLIVE 35 aircraft at 0.9 points, no wifi 131 aircraft at 0.0 points. Added up that is ConnectScore 48, and the Starlink row on its own is the next-gen number, 31. The full method, the three confidence tiers and the things it cannot see are on the methodology page.

Can you tell me whether my specific flight will have Starlink?

For United, yes: we hold a per-flight history and can give the odds for that flight number. For Alaska the tails are verified but there is no per-flight feed, so the best we can do is the sub-fleet. For every other airline all we have is the fleet-wide score, and we say so rather than inventing a number for one flight.
