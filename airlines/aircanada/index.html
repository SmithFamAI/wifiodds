Air Canada WiFi questions

Does Air Canada have Starlink WiFi?

Air Canada has Starlink on 12 of its 216 aircraft (6%) as of 2026-07, so it is still unlikely on a random flight. Its ConnectScore is 6 out of 100 — rare.

Is Air Canada’s WiFi free?

On Air Canada it is free for loyalty members. ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor, so a paid or unconfirmed free claim scores lower than the same fleet given away free.
