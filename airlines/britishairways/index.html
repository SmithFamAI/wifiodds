British Airways WiFi questions

Does British Airways have Starlink WiFi?

British Airways has Starlink on 5 of its 261 aircraft (2%) as of 2026-07, so it is still unlikely on a random flight. Its ConnectScore is 15 out of 100, in the rare band.

Is British Airways’s WiFi free?

On British Airways it is free onboard. ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor, so a paid or unconfirmed free claim scores lower than the same fleet given away free.

How many British Airways planes have Starlink?

5 of 261, or 2% of the fleet. Compiled from public airline announcements, July 2026.

ConnectScore is an expected value over a whole fleet, not a prediction about one flight: United measured 320, 56 and 15 Mbps on three systems in one livery in one reporting period. Aircraft whose system an airline does not publish are left out of the denominator rather than assumed. Signed-but-unflown deals (AA Starlink 2027, DL/B6 Amazon Leo) score zero until they fly.
