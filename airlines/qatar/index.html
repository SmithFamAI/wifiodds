The playbook

What to do about it

Four things, in the order they become useful. Every one of them is available at tier C, which is the tier this page was derived at. Anything this fleet cannot support has been cut from the list.

  • T-48hRe-check about two days out

    Aircraft assignments firm up roughly 48 hours before departure and they can still move up to pushback. Whatever this page says today, the answer for your flight is more settled then.

  • AmenityRead the airline’s own WiFi flag at T-48h

    Qatar Airways publishes an amenity marker against each flight in its own booking flow, and that marker is about the aircraft now assigned to it. It is the closest thing to a per-flight answer this fleet has. The airline is asserting it; nobody has verified the install.

  • SwitchPrice the same-day change before you need it

    Roughly 58% of the fleet carries Starlink today, so a different departure is a real second draw. Whether that is worth doing is a question about the change fee on your fare, and it is cheaper to know the answer before the gate than at it.

  • FallbackHave the offline version of the work

    Nobody has load-tested a full cabin on any of these systems, so a good score is not a promise about the hour you fly. Downloading the deck is the move that makes the odds stop mattering.

Qatar Airways WiFi questions

Does Qatar Airways have Starlink WiFi?

Qatar Airways has Starlink on 140 of its 241 aircraft (58%) as of 2026-07, so it is better than a coin flip. Its ConnectScore is 61 out of 100, in the good band.

Is Qatar Airways’s WiFi free?

On Qatar Airways it is free onboard. ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor, so a paid or unconfirmed free claim scores lower than the same fleet given away free.
