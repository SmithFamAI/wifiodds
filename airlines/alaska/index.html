Alaska WiFi questions

Does Alaska have Starlink WiFi?

Alaska has Starlink on 99 of its 350 aircraft (28%) as of 2026-07, so it is a real possibility but not the default. Its ConnectScore is 28 out of 100 — long shot.

Is Alaska’s WiFi free?

On Alaska it is free onboard. ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor, so a paid or unconfirmed free claim scores lower than the same fleet given away free.

How many Alaska planes have Starlink?

99 of 350 — 28% of the fleet. Verified tail by tail by alaskastarlinktracker.com (@martinamps).

ConnectScore is the chance of getting the GOOD system, not of any wifi at all. Legacy satellite service on the not-yet-converted part of a fleet is not credited. Signed-but-unflown deals (AA Starlink 2027, DL/B6 Amazon Leo) score zero until they fly.
