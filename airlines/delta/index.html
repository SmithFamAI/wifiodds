Delta WiFi questions

Does Delta have Viasat WiFi?

Delta offers Viasat fleetwide as of 2026-07. Its ConnectScore is 49 out of 100, in the mixed band.

Is Delta’s WiFi free?

On Delta it is free onboard. ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor, so a paid or unconfirmed free claim scores lower than the same fleet given away free.
