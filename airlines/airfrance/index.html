Air France WiFi questions

Does Air France have Starlink WiFi?

Air France has Starlink on 172 of its 229 aircraft (75%) as of 2026-07, so it is better than a coin flip. Its ConnectScore is 80 out of 100 — good.

Is Air France’s WiFi free?

On Air France it is free onboard. ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor, so a paid or unconfirmed free claim scores lower than the same fleet given away free.
