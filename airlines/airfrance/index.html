Air France WiFi questions

Does Air France have Starlink WiFi?

Air France has Starlink on 172 of its 229 aircraft (75%) as of 2026-07, so it is better than a coin flip. Its ConnectScore is 80 out of 100, in the good band.

Is Air France’s WiFi free?

On Air France it is free onboard. ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor, so a paid or unconfirmed free claim scores lower than the same fleet given away free.

How many Air France planes have Starlink?

ConnectScore is an expected value over a whole fleet, not a prediction about one flight: United measured 320, 56 and 15 Mbps on three systems in one livery in one reporting period. Aircraft whose system an airline does not publish are left out of the denominator rather than assumed. Signed-but-unflown deals (AA Starlink 2027, DL/B6 Amazon Leo) score zero until they fly.
