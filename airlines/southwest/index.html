Does Southwest have Starlink WiFi?

Is Southwest’s WiFi free?

On Southwest it is free for loyalty members. ConnectScore multiplies the fleet share by a free-for-you factor, so a paid or unconfirmed free claim scores lower than the same fleet given away free.
