04 / What the numbers mean

Two views. Two honest answers.

Start with the chance of Starlink or Amazon Leo. If that is not available, switch views to find airlines with confirmed streaming-capable WiFi, and see when the remaining fleet is unknown.

Streaming or better

The confirmed share with next-gen or modern streaming WiFi. “≥” means the rest of the fleet is genuinely unknown.

ConnectScore

A 0–100 rating of the WiFi available today across the whole fleet, blending every fitted system and whether access is free.
