The extension answers the page you are already looking at: every United result picks up a colour-coded Starlink odds badge, and one click sorts the whole page by them — without leaving the booking flow. Below is what that looks like, in real screenshots rather than a mockup.

Real screenshots · v1.5.1united.com · app.navan.com
  1. 1Search

    You search united.com the way you always do. The extension adds nothing to the page until there are results on it — and it never touches the booking or payment steps.

  2. 2Odds

    Every result picks up its own badge: how often that flight number has actually drawn a Starlink aircraft. 49% here, 44% on the next one — and a ✓ instead of a percentage once the assigned tail is confirmed equipped.

  3. 3Sort

    One click reorders the whole page best-odds-first — prices and times untouched — so you can see what the good connection actually costs you. Same panel on Navan as on united.com.

  4. 4Guard

    The panel is straight about what it cannot know yet: tails firm up around 48 hours out. Watching that window after you book is the Tail-swap Guardian — built, in test, shipping in 2.1.

How ConnectScore works

0–100

ConnectScore = P(connectivity) × system quality × free-for-you

ConnectScore is the chance of getting the GOOD system, not of any wifi at all. Legacy satellite service on the not-yet-converted part of a fleet is not credited. Signed-but-unflown deals (AA Starlink 2027, DL/B6 Amazon Leo) score zero until they fly.
