How ConnectScore works

0–100
ConnectScore = P(connectivity) × system quality × free-for-you
P(connectivity)

The share of the fleet actually carrying the system today — equipped aircraft divided by total aircraft, from the trackers where they exist and from airline announcements otherwise.

System quality

Starlink and Amazon Leo (low-earth orbit) count 1.0. Viasat, Intelsat and Gogo 2Ku count 0.6. Older geostationary systems count 0.3.

Free for you

Free for everyone, or free with a free-to-join loyalty program, counts 1.0. A paid status tier, a partial rollout, or an unconfirmed free claim counts 0.85. Paid WiFi counts 0.7.
