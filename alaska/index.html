Alaska Airlines · Starlink rollout

Alaska is installing Starlink faster than almost anyone, and it's free for everyone onboard — no loyalty program, no tier, no purchase. The catch is the same as everywhere else: it's aircraft by aircraft, so which jet you get decides your day. The regional fleet is already finished; mainline is only just starting.

Per-flight odds while you book

Alaska support lives in the extension

There's no route optimizer for Alaska on this site yet. What exists today is better placed anyway — inside the booking flow. Install WiFi Odds for Flights and enable the optional alaskaair.com permission, and you get:

  • Odds badges on Alaska search results, per flight, colour-coded the same as United
  • The Alaska ConnectScore and fleet split in the popup, wherever you are
  • Nothing sent anywhere but a flight number and a date — no accounts, no analytics, no tracking
