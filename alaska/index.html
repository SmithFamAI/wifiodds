§3 · Questions

Alaska wifi, answered

Does Alaska have Starlink wifi?

Yes, on 99 of 350 aircraft (28%): the whole E175 regional fleet plus the ex-Hawaiian widebodies, with the mainline 737s only just starting. Counted from the alaskastarlinktracker.com roster, 2026-07-25.

Is Alaska's wifi free?

Where Starlink is installed it is free for everyone onboard, with no loyalty programme, tier or purchase. That is why Alaska's free-for-you factor is the maximum 1.00 in the ConnectScore arithmetic above.

How many Alaska planes have Starlink?

99 of 350, checked tail by tail by alaskastarlinktracker.com (@martinamps) and read on 2026-07-25.

Which Alaska flight should I book for wifi?

A regional flight on an E175 if the schedule offers one, because that sub-fleet is finished. On mainline, check the specific tail at T-48h. Aircraft assignments publish about two days out and they still change after that.
