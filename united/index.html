United · Route optimizer

Every United flight on your route, ranked by Starlink odds

Give it a city pair. The page ranks each flight number by how often that departure has drawn a Starlink-equipped tail, scores the connections against the non-stops, lists the tails already assigned for the next 72 hours, and ends with the four moves that turn a coin flip into a booking.

484 of 1,807 United aircraft carry Starlink as of 2026-07-28. That line is baked in at build time, so it is right before any of the live sections below have loaded. See every equipped tail.

United's own count: 450 of 1,552 (29.0%), as of 30 Jun 2026, from United Airlines Holdings, Inc.'s Second-Quarter 2026 Results news release. It counts fewer regional tails than the tracker does, so the two figures are never added or divided together.

38 pairs with flight history · 191 departures

The optimizer asks the tracker for a route while you are looking at it, so it needs JavaScript. These tables do not. They are the last answer the tracker gave for every route in this page's data file, baked in when the site was built, and reading them sends nothing anywhere. Every row is a mainline United flight number, the share of its observed departures that drew a Starlink-equipped tail, the number of departures behind that share, the tracker's confidence word and the date the answer was cached. Pairs are ordered by the highest rate on the pair among departures with at least 5 observations.

Below 5 observed departures the rate is not printed. A flight seen three times can read 100% or 0% and neither figure tells you anything, so those rows say history too thin to lean on instead. 29 of the 191 rows here fall below that floor. The confidence word is the tracker's own and it does not follow the observation count: low covers 1 to 14 observations, medium covers 3 to 21, high covers 10 to 41, so the two columns are worth reading against each other.

§4 · The playbook

Four moves, booking to boarding

This part works on any route and it is the part that actually moves your odds. The T-48h check is worth more than the booking choice.

BOOK

Book the high-probability flight

Book the departure with the longest Starlink history on your pair and keep the next two as backups. Avoid the departures that usually draw 737 MAX, A319, A320 or 757 equipment. Those four fleets have no Starlink installs at all today. The cached tables at the top of this page rank every route the tracker has answered for, and each row carries the number of departures behind it.

T-24H

Re-check, and switch if you drew a dud

Swaps happen. If your flight lost its Starlink tail, look for another departure on the same pair that has one: the confirmed-departures list in §2.2 names the tails already published for your route, and the tracker's own route page carries the same list. A same-day change costs nothing on Gold and above, and $75 otherwise, waived to standby. That is how a coin flip becomes a near-certainty.

FLY

Connect with MileagePlus

Starlink is free onboard, and the sign-in is a MileagePlus account, so make sure everyone travelling with you has one. Expect roughly 100 to 250 Mbps gate to gate.
